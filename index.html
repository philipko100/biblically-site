Read Scripture. Keep the habit.

Biblically is a Bible study app for iPhone. Read chapter by chapter, highlight what stays with you, keep a prayer journal, and let Daily Focus hold back the apps pulling you away until you have read.

What it does

  • The whole library. The World English Bible, a public-domain modern translation, in the Catholic, Orthodox, or Protestant canon — your choice.
  • Highlights and notes. Mark a verse or a range, attach a thought, find it again in Saved.
  • A prayer journal. Write freely, day by day, with a calendar showing where you have been.
  • Daily Focus. Choose the apps that distract you and they stay shielded until you have spent time reading. Your choices never leave your iPhone.
