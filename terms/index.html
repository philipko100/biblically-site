What Biblically is

Biblically is a Bible reading and prayer app for iPhone. It lets you:

  • Read the World English Bible in the Catholic, Orthodox, or Protestant canon
  • Bookmark chapters, highlight verses, attach notes, and save favourite passages
  • Keep a prayer journal and record a short daily check-in
  • Use Daily Focus, which shields apps you choose until you have spent time reading
  • See a verse of the day, in the app and as a Home or Lock Screen widget
  • Ask for a short explanation of a passage you have selected, which is generated by a third-party AI model
