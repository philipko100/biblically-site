Privacy Policy

Effective 2 September 2026

Biblically is a Bible reading and prayer journal app. This policy explains what the app stores, what it sends elsewhere, and how to delete all of it. There are no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind in this app.

What we collect

Your account

Signing in is required, and is handled by Apple or Google. We receive and store your email address and, where the provider supplies it, your name. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose Hide My Email, we only ever receive Apple's private relay address — we never see your real one.

What you create in the app

Stored against your account so it syncs across your devices:

  • Bookmarked chapters, highlighted verses, and any notes you attach to them
  • Favourited passages
  • Prayer journal entries
  • Daily check-in answers (how connected you feel, and how you are feeling, each on a five-point scale) and whether you read or journaled that day
  • Your answers to the short questions asked when you first create an account
  • Your tradition setting, which decides which canon is shown

What stays on your device

Never uploaded anywhere:

  • Chapters you have read, cached so they work offline
  • Your reading position and display preferences
  • Your Daily Focus app selection — see below

What we never collect

No analytics or telemetry SDKs are present in the app at all. We do not collect location, contacts, photos, health data, or your device advertising identifier. We do not show ads, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone for advertising or marketing.

Daily Focus and Screen Time

Daily Focus shields distracting apps until you have read. It is built on Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework, and Apple's design means the app cannot see which apps you chose — the system hands back an opaque token, not a list. That token is stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own private storage. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we could not read it if it were.

Deleting your account

Open the app, go to Me → Account, and tap Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent: your account and every record described above are removed from our database, and the copy on your device is erased at the same time. There is no recovery period and we keep no backup copy of your content. You do not need to contact us.

Signing out, by contrast, leaves your data intact so you can sign back in.

Keeping data

Your content is kept until you delete your account or delete the individual items. Explanation text generated for a passage is cached without any link to the person who requested it, so it is not personal data and remains after an account is deleted.

Children

Biblically is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can see everything the app holds about you inside the app itself, correct it by editing it, and delete all of it with the button described above. Depending on where you live you may have further rights over your personal data; write to us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the effective date above and, where the change affects how your data is used, tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: privacy@biblically.app
